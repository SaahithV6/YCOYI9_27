How it works

01Every miss, recorded1,502 non-launches with the weather observed at the pad, cause labels with evidence grades, and 525 archived Space Force forecasts.
02Causes recoveredValidated enrichment and quote-checked web research recover causes the record never published.
03Weather modelGradient-boosted trees on archived forecasts at each attempt's hour: trained on what a forecast said, served on a forecast. No LLM, zero tokens.
04Rules, honestlyThe 10 lightning rules and user constraints. A forecast can fire a rule, never clear one; missing instruments are named.
05Memory that learnsEvery mission lives in GBrain with sourced facts; corrections feed the next run.
Data: Launch Library 2 update stream, KXMR METAR, GOES GLM, NEXRAD, IGRA soundings, archived 45th Weather Squadron forecasts, Open-Meteo live and archived forecasts, NOAA SWPC. A forecast is never graded as an observation; a model score is a ranked risk, not a probability of scrub.
